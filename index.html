Hello! Welcome to my website :)

I’m a third-year PhD student studying Mathematics at Duke University. I am fortunate to be advised by Dr. Veronica Ciocanel.

I am interested in building mechanistic models to understand the behavior of biological systems over time. I'm currently developing an agent-based model of microtubule dynamics in neurons. I'm also involved with MISM, where I work with Drs. Cliburn Chan and Jessica Conway on modeling HIV dynamics.

I graduated from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC) in 2024, where I was advised by Dr. Brad Peercy. Go Retrievers!
